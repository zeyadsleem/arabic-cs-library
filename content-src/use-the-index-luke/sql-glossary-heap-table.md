---
title: "Heap-Table"
lang: en
---

A heap-table stores the rows in an unordered fashion. [Index-organized tables](/sql/glossary/index-organized-table) and [clustered indexes](/sql/glossary/clustered-index) are the alternative. They store the rows in an ordered [B-Tree](/sql/anatomy/the-tree) structure.
